How RideMate Works

RideMate is a smart, hostel-based cab sharing platform designed to make student travel safer, more affordable, and more organized. This page explains the complete working of the RideMate system from registration to ride completion.

Step 1: User Registration

New users must register on the RideMate platform using a valid college email address. This verification step ensures that only genuine students are allowed to access the platform.

During registration, users are required to set a secure password and provide basic personal information. Once registration is successful, the user account becomes active.

Step 2: Login & Authentication

Registered users can log in to RideMate using their email and password. The system verifies the credentials and grants access to the dashboard only if the details are correct.

This authentication mechanism prevents unauthorized access and keeps user data secure at all times.

Step 3: Profile Completion

After logging in for the first time, users are encouraged to complete their profile by adding accurate details such as name, hostel ID, contact information, and preferred travel locations.

A complete and updated profile builds trust among users and helps drivers and riders communicate effectively.

Step 4: Search for a Ride

Users who are looking for a ride can search for available cab rides by entering the source location, destination, date of travel, and preferred time.

RideMate displays a list of matching rides along with driver details, vehicle information, available seats, and estimated fare.

Step 5: Post a Ride

Users who are organizing or driving a cab can post a new ride by entering details such as route, pickup point, drop location, date, time, and number of available seats.

This feature helps other students join the ride and share travel costs efficiently.

Step 6: Booking & Confirmation

After selecting a suitable ride, users can send a booking request to the driver. The booking status is initially marked as "Pending".

Once the driver approves the request, the booking status changes to "Confirmed" and the ride appears in the user’s ride history.

Step 7: Ride Day & Communication

On the day of travel, riders can contact the driver using the contact details provided within the platform for coordination.

RideMate ensures transparency by displaying driver and vehicle details clearly before the journey begins.

Step 8: Safety & Monitoring

Safety is a core feature of RideMate. All users are verified using college email authentication to maintain a secure and trusted community.

Ride history, user details, and booking records are stored securely to ensure accountability and traceability.

Step 9: Ride Completion & History

After the ride is completed, users can view the ride details in their history section for future reference.

This record helps users track travel patterns, expenses, and past bookings conveniently.

Step 10: Continuous Improvement

RideMate continuously works on improving the platform based on user feedback and system analysis.

User suggestions help enhance safety, usability, and overall travel experience for the student community.
