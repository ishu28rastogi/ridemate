About RideMate

RideMate is a cab-sharing web application designed to make daily travel easier, safer, and more affordable for hostel students. The platform helps students connect with others traveling to the same destination and share rides efficiently.

The Problem We Faced

Hostel students often face difficulties while booking cabs or autos. Students traveling to the same location usually book separate rides, which increases travel costs. In addition, students face several other issues:

  • High travel expenses due to individual bookings
  • Difficulty in finding reliable and trusted ride partners
  • Safety concerns, especially during late hours
  • Lack of proper coordination among students
  • Existing cab apps do not focus on hostel-specific needs

Our Solution

To overcome these challenges, we developed RideMate. RideMate provides a secure and student-focused platform where only registered hostel students can create and join rides.

  • Students can create rides by specifying destination, time, and available seats
  • Other students traveling on the same route can search and join rides
  • Fare sharing helps reduce overall travel cost
  • User authentication ensures safety and trust
  • Improved coordination through a centralized ride-sharing system

RideMate aims to provide a smart, safe, and cost-effective cab-sharing solution that simplifies travel for hostel students while promoting collaboration and sustainability.
