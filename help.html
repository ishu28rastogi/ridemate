Help Centre

Welcome to RideMate Help Centre. Our goal is to ensure that every user has a safe, smooth, and reliable experience while using our cab-sharing platform. Whether you are new to RideMate or a regular user, this page will guide you through common questions and usage instructions.

Getting Started

RideMate is a hostel-based cab sharing platform designed exclusively for college students. The platform helps students share rides safely, reduce travel costs, and promote a trusted community environment.

To get started, new users must register using a valid college email ID. This ensures that only verified students can access the platform. After completing the registration process, users can log in using their email and password.

Booking a Ride

Booking a ride on RideMate is simple and user-friendly. Users can search for available rides by entering their source location, destination, preferred date of travel.

RideMate displays a list of available rides posted by verified drivers. Each ride includes important details such as driver number, vehicle number, pickup time, available seats, and estimated fare.

After selecting a suitable ride, users can confirm their booking. The booking status will initially appear as "Pending" and will change to "Confirmed" once the driver approves the request. All booked rides can be viewed in the History section for future reference.

Safety & Security

Safety and security are the top priorities of RideMate. To maintain a trusted platform, all users are verified through their college email IDs during the registration process.

Driver information, including personal details, vehicle registration number, and ride history, is securely stored in the system. This ensures transparency and accountability for every ride offered on the platform.

Users are encouraged to review driver details before confirming a ride and to report any suspicious activity immediately. RideMate continuously monitors platform usage to provide a secure travel experience for all users.

Ride History

RideMate aims to make ride-sharing affordable and convenient for students. Fare estimates, and seat availability are clearly displayed before booking a ride.

Users can view their complete ride history under the History section. This includes details of past, ongoing, and cancelled rides along with payment status and timestamps.

Maintaining ride history helps users keep track of their travel expenses and provides useful data for resolving disputes or issues related to rides.
